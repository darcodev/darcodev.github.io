//02. about me

I’m a high school senior. I build small Python command line tools to solve problems I actually run into, with no extra dependencies, real docs, and an MIT license. A lot come out of my reselling side business, like an image search that identifies a product and finds where it’s selling. That side business eventually got its own platform: navar.app, an inventory and sales manager for sneaker and card resellers.

When I’m not coding I’m on the bench with a microcontroller. That led to two finished builds, an LCD temperature alarm I use for car detailing and a two player reaction game. Demos are in projects.
